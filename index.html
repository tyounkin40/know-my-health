Know My Health

Patients tell stories. Doctors want data. This page translates between the two.

It records what you report. It never diagnoses or recommends treatment.

How to use it

  1. The page opens with an example. To tell your own story, click Start fresh in step 2 below.
  2. Mark where it hurts. In step 1, click the spot on the body. Click Back to turn the body around. Click the same spot again to clear it.
  3. Pinpoint fingers, toes, or joints. Click a hand or foot on the body, or click R hand L hand R foot L foot. Then click the finger, toe, or circle (joint).
  4. Say how bad it is. Under the body, drag the How bad, 0 to 10 slider. Then click every word that fits, such as Aching or Burning. To change an area later, click its name in Marked areas.
  5. Tell your story. In step 2, type in the box. Or click Speak, talk, and click Stop listening when you finish.
  6. Translate it. Click Translate my story. Wait 10 to 20 seconds. Your results appear in step 3.
  7. Read your results. In step 3, click each tab: For you Clinical note Story → data Doctor's questions.
  8. Answer the doctor's questions. Click Doctor's questions, type your answers, click Add my answers to the story, then click Translate my story again.
  9. Fill in the summary details. In step 4, type the Patient name and Date of birth. Under Completed by, choose who is filling this in. If it's not the patient, type that person's name.
  10. Take it to the doctor. Click Download summary (PDF). Open the file to print it, or show it on your phone at the visit.
  11. Optional: log an episode between visits. In step 3, click Between visits, then Save today's entry.

    4 · Carry it forward

    Take it to your doctor

    Download a one-to-two page summary to print or show on your phone: body maps with your marks, your story in your own words, the clinical history, the questions your doctor may ask (with space to write answers), and any episodes you logged. A caregiver can bring it on your behalf.

    About this summary

    Printed at the top of the summary and included in the FHIR record. Kept only in this browser.

    Continue with your own records

    If you've connected your health records in a consumer AI app, take this record there to add your history and current medications. The prompt asks the app not to diagnose or suggest treatment. ChatGPT and Claude open with it prefilled; for Gemini, it's copied so you can paste it.
